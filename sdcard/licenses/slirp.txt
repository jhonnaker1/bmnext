SLiRP and Previous's network servers
====================================

BMNeXT's network for the NeXT is SLiRP (Previous's src/slirp/), a NAT
router, with Previous's own NFS, NetInfo, portmap, bootparam and DNS
servers (src/slirp/rpc/), built into the kernels. Their notices follow, as
in their source files.
